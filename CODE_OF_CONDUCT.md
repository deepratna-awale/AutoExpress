# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind and respectful to other people,
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences,
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
- taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them,
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances,
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks,
- public or private harassment,
- publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission,
- posting images of real people without their consent,
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Images shared in issues, pull requests and discussions must be safe for work.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a [private report](https://github.com/deepratna-awale/AutoExpress/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@deepratna-awale](https://github.com/deepratna-awale) on GitHub. Every report is reviewed promptly and handled privately.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
